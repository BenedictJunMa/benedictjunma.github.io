Mr. Luiz Fernando RAMOS LEMOS
---
Visiting Research Student in INTR (Sept 2026 - present)

Luiz Fernando Ramos Lemos is a Visiting Research Student at the iLSCA Lab, HKUST(GZ). He received his B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Applied and Computational Mathematics from the University of Campinas (UNICAMP), Brazil, and is currently pursuing his Ph.D. degree in Production Engineering at the Federal University of Itajubá (UNIFEI), Brazil. He is also a Professor at the Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Southern Minas Gerais (IFSULDEMINAS), Brazil. His research interests include linear programming, mixed-integer linear programming, artificial intelligence for decision support, and computer vision for industrial quality inspection.

Representative publication:

Lemos, L. F. R., Soares, A. C., Cesário, M. C., Francisco, M. B., & Montevechi, J. A. B. (2026). Comparative Study of Cosine-Histogram Association and Deep Learning Methods for Leather Defect Detection. IEEE Access. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3672385)
